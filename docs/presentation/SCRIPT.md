## Recording notes

- 16:9, 1080p; OBS or Xbox Game Bar (`Win+G`) both fine. Hide the mouse
  cursor except when clicking is the point.
- Fullscreen the browser (F11) — no tab bar, no bookmarks.
- Record gameplay in ONE session after Checkpoint 3; keep clips as separate
  files (A/B/C) so you can trim to the timeline.
- Voice: record narration separately if the room is noisy; the timings above
  are ~2 s of slack per segment.
- Slides: present fullscreen, hard cuts to gameplay at the `>>>` markers —
  no transitions needed.
